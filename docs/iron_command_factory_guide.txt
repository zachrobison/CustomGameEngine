IRON COMMAND — COSTS, FACTORY STATS, AND FACTORY DESIGNS
========================================================

This document covers the numbers behind Iron Command's economy: what each item
costs in raw ore, how fast each machine produces, how many machines to balance a
production line, and some example factory layouts. All figures come from the
game's recipes. Each section is self-contained.


1. RESOURCE COST REFERENCE (RAW ORE PER ITEM)
---------------------------------------------
Every item traces back to raw ore. These are the cumulative costs — the total
ore needed to make one of each item, counting every refining step.

- 1 Ore = 1 ore (mined directly).
- 1 Ingot = 1 ore (smelted from 1 ore).
- 1 Screw = 0.5 ore (a Constructor makes 2 screws from 1 ingot, so each screw is
  half an ingot).
- 1 Plate = 1 ore (1 ingot makes 1 plate).
- 1 Rod = 1 ore (1 ingot makes 1 rod).
- 1 Part = 1.5 ore if made from a Rod + Screw, or 2 ore if made from a Plate + 2
  Screws. The Rod recipe is the cheaper way to make Parts.
- 1 Robot = 4 ore (2 Plates + 4 Screws = 2 ore + 2 ore). In ingot terms, a Robot
  is 4 ingots: 2 ingots for the plates and 2 ingots for the four screws.
- 1 Turret = 2 ore (1 Plate + 1 Rod).
- 1 Mine = 2 ore (1 Plate + 2 Screws).
- 1 Fence = 2 ore (2 Rods).
- 1 Tripwire = 1.5 ore (1 Rod + 1 Screw).

Cheapest defense per unit: the Tripwire (1.5 ore). The Robot (4 ore) is the most
expensive single item in the game.


2. PRODUCTION RATES (THROUGHPUT PER MACHINE)
--------------------------------------------
Each machine's output rate is its recipe output divided by its craft time. "/s"
means per second; "/min" means per minute.

- Miner (Mine Ore): 1 ore every 1.4 s = about 0.71 ore/s = ~43 ore/min.
- Miner (Overclock): 2 ore every 1.4 s = about 1.43 ore/s = ~86 ore/min.
- Miner (Turbo): 3 ore every 1.2 s = 2.5 ore/s = 150 ore/min.
- Smelter: 1 ingot every 2.0 s = 0.5 ingot/s = 30 ingot/min.
- Constructor (Screws): 2 screws every 1.5 s = ~1.33 screw/s = ~80 screw/min.
- Constructor (Plate): 1 plate every 2.0 s = 0.5 plate/s = 30 plate/min.
- Constructor (Rod): 1 rod every 1.2 s = ~0.83 rod/s = ~50 rod/min.
- Assembler (Part from Plate): 1 part every 3.0 s = ~0.33 part/s = 20 part/min.
- Assembler (Part from Rod): 1 part every 2.5 s = 0.4 part/s = 24 part/min.
- Assembler (Robot): 1 robot every 4.0 s = 0.25 robot/s = 15 robot/min.
- Assembler (Turret): 1 every 3.5 s. Assembler (Mine): 1 every 2.5 s.
  Assembler (Fence): 1 every 2.0 s. Assembler (Tripwire): 1 every 2.0 s.

One Assembler on the Robot recipe produces 15 robots per minute at full feed.


3. BALANCING A LINE (MACHINE RATIOS)
------------------------------------
To run a machine at full speed you must feed it fast enough. These ratios keep
each stage supplied with no starving and no overflow.

Robot line (to keep one Assembler making Robots at full speed):
- 1 Assembler on the Robot recipe needs 0.5 plate/s and 1 screw/s.
- Plates: 1 Constructor on Plate makes exactly 0.5 plate/s. -> 1 Plate Constructor.
- Screws: 1 Constructor on Screws makes ~1.33 screw/s, more than the 1 screw/s
  needed. -> 1 Screw Constructor (with headroom).
- Ingots: the two Constructors together consume about 1 ingot/s.
- Smelters: each Smelter makes 0.5 ingot/s, so 1 ingot/s needs 2 Smelters.
- Ore: 1 ingot/s needs 1 ore/s. One Overclock Miner (1.43 ore/s) covers it, or
  two basic Miners.

SUMMARY RATIO for a full Robot line:
1 Assembler : 1 Plate Constructor : 1 Screw Constructor : 2 Smelters :
1 Overclock Miner (or ~1.5 basic Miners). Output: 15 robots per minute.

Rule of thumb: 1 Smelter feeds roughly 1 Constructor; 2 Smelters feed the pair of
Constructors that feed 1 Robot Assembler.


4. EXAMPLE FACTORY DESIGNS
--------------------------

DESIGN A — Starter Robot Line (minimum viable army)
A compact build you can throw up early to start making robots:
- 2 Miners on the nearest ore deposit (or 1 set to Overclock).
- 2 Smelters fed by the miners.
- 2 Constructors: one set to Plate, one set to Screws.
- 1 Assembler on the Robot recipe.
- 1 Barracks to stockpile finished robots.
- Conveyor belts: Miners -> Smelters -> Constructors -> Assembler -> Barracks.
Result: about 15 robots per minute. March them from the Hub Command Map.

DESIGN B — Defense Line (turtle up)
To wall off and defend your Hub instead of attacking:
- Same ore -> ingot -> component base as Design A.
- Point the Assembler at defense recipes instead of Robots: Fences (2 Rods),
  Mines (1 Plate + 2 Screws), and Turrets (1 Plate + 1 Rod).
- Add a Constructor on the Rod recipe, since Fences and Turrets need Rods.
- Defenses accumulate in a stockpile; open the Hub Command Map and DRAW them:
  drag fence walls across chokepoints, paint minefields on approaches, and place
  turret clusters covering the gaps.

DESIGN C — Mass-Robot Scale-Up (for aggression)
To flood the map with robots, duplicate the Robot line:
- Each full Robot line = 15 robots/min. Two lines = 30/min, three = 45/min.
- Each added line needs its own 1 Assembler + 1 Plate Constructor + 1 Screw
  Constructor + 2 Smelters + ~1 Overclock Miner.
- Mining is usually the first bottleneck: switch Miners to Overclock or Turbo,
  or claim more ore deposits with more Miners.


5. EFFICIENCY TIPS
------------------
- Make Parts from Rods (1.5 ore) rather than Plates (2 ore) when you only need
  Parts for upgrades — it is 25% cheaper in ore.
- Overclock or Turbo your Miners before adding more Smelters; mining is the
  usual first bottleneck.
- Keep belts short and the chain clustered near your Hub so robots and defenses
  deploy close to what they protect.
- A Robot costs 4 ore and 4 ingots — the most expensive item — so protect your
  Barracks stockpile and do not throw robots away carelessly.
- Build defenses (fences, mines, turrets) in advance; they sit in the stockpile
  until you draw them, so you can bank them during quiet moments.


6. QUICK STATS SUMMARY (FOR RETRIEVAL)
--------------------------------------
- Robot cost: 4 ore (2 Plates + 4 Screws = 4 ingots). Most expensive item.
- Cheapest defense: Tripwire at 1.5 ore. Turret/Mine/Fence are 2 ore each.
- Cheapest Part: Rod + Screw = 1.5 ore.
- One Robot Assembler = 15 robots/min at full feed.
- Full Robot line ratio: 1 Assembler : 1 Plate Constructor : 1 Screw
  Constructor : 2 Smelters : 1 Overclock Miner.
- Miner output: 0.71 ore/s (Mine Ore), 1.43 ore/s (Overclock), 2.5 ore/s (Turbo).
- Smelter: 0.5 ingot/s. Plate Constructor: 0.5 plate/s. Screw Constructor:
  ~1.33 screw/s.
