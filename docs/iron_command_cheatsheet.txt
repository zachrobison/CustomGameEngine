IRON COMMAND — QUICK-REFERENCE CHEAT SHEET (COMBINED FACTS)
==========================================================

Each entry below bundles the facts that usually go together, so a single
retrieved entry can answer a multi-part question on its own. All numbers match
the game's rules and recipes.


ROBOT (all-in-one): A Robot is the mobile combat unit. Recipe: 2 Plates +
4 Screws at the Assembler. Craft time: 4 seconds. Cost: 4 ore (equal to 4
ingots) — the most expensive item in the game. Output: one Assembler makes
15 robots per minute. Full production line to feed it: 1 Assembler + 1 Plate
Constructor + 1 Screw Constructor + 2 Smelters + 1 Overclock Miner.

TURRET (all-in-one): A Turret is an automated gun that fires on nearby enemies.
Recipe: 1 Plate + 1 Rod at the Assembler. Craft time: 3.5 seconds. Cost: 2 ore.
It is stockpiled, then placed by dragging a box on the Hub Command Map.

MINE (all-in-one): A Mine is an area trap. Recipe: 1 Plate + 2 Screws at the
Assembler. Craft time: 2.5 seconds. Cost: 2 ore. It detonates on any hostile
robot (an enemy's, never your own) for heavy area damage. Stockpiled, then
painted as a minefield by dragging a box on the Hub Command Map.

FENCE (all-in-one): A Fence is a tall wall that physically blocks robots.
Recipe: 2 Rods at the Assembler. Craft time: 2.0 seconds. Cost: 2 ore.
Stockpiled, then drawn as a wall by dragging on the Hub Command Map.

TRIPWIRE (all-in-one): A Tripwire is a trap that triggers on a passing enemy.
Recipe: 1 Rod + 1 Screw at the Assembler. Craft time: 2.0 seconds. Cost:
1.5 ore — the cheapest defense.

PART (all-in-one): A Part is an advanced component and the game's upgrade
currency. Two recipes at the Assembler: 1 Rod + 1 Screw (costs 1.5 ore, the
cheaper option) or 1 Plate + 2 Screws (costs 2 ore).

ITEM COSTS (all-in-one): Cumulative raw-ore cost of every item — Ore: 1,
Ingot: 1, Screw: 0.5, Plate: 1, Rod: 1, Part: 1.5 or 2, Robot: 4, Turret: 2,
Mine: 2, Fence: 2, Tripwire: 1.5. Most expensive: Robot (4 ore). Cheapest
defense: Tripwire (1.5 ore).

PRODUCTION RATES (all-in-one): Miner — 0.71 ore/s (Mine Ore), 1.43 ore/s
(Overclock), 2.5 ore/s (Turbo). Smelter — 0.5 ingot/s. Constructor — 1.33
screw/s (Screws), 0.5 plate/s (Plate), 0.83 rod/s (Rod). Assembler — 0.25
robot/s (15/min), 0.33–0.4 part/s. One Assembler on Robots = 15 robots/minute.

ROBOT LINE RATIO (all-in-one): To run one Robot Assembler at full speed:
1 Assembler : 1 Plate Constructor : 1 Screw Constructor : 2 Smelters :
1 Overclock Miner (or ~2 basic Miners). Output: 15 robots per minute. First
bottleneck is usually mining — overclock the Miners before scaling.

MACHINES (all-in-one): Miner (on an ore deposit, makes ore), Smelter (ore ->
ingot), Constructor (ingot -> screws/plate/rod), Assembler (makes Parts, Robots,
and all defenses), Barracks (stores robots), Terminal (opens the Command Map),
Hub (premade home base, 1400 health), Conveyor belt (moves items, auto
split/merge).

WIN AND LOSS (all-in-one): You win by being the last competitor standing — all
rival Hubs destroyed while yours survives. You lose only if your own Hub is
destroyed. If your character's health hits zero you respawn at your Hub at full
health; player death is never a loss.

HUB (all-in-one): The Hub is your home base. It is premade and auto-placed at
your chosen drop point (clear of ore), and you spawn beside it. It has 1400
health. Losing it eliminates you. Standing next to it and pressing E opens the
top-down Command Map used to command robots and draw defenses.

COMMAND MAP (all-in-one): Opened at the Hub or any Terminal (press E). A flat
top-down tactical view with four tools: March (click/drag to send all robots,
including into unexplored areas), Fence (drag a wall), Mines (drag a box to
paint a minefield), and Turrets (drag a box to place a turret cluster).

MULTIPLAYER (all-in-one): A free-for-all with no AI enemies — your only foes are
other human players. One player clicks Host (the game shows that player's local
network IP address); others click Join and type that address. Port: 49813. All
players must be on the same local network (same Wi-Fi/router); internet play
needs an external tool (virtual network, port-forwarding, or a relay server).

CONTROLS (all-in-one): WASD = move; mouse = look/aim; left mouse = fire the ray
gun or place a building; B = toggle Build mode; right mouse = dismantle a
building in Build mode; E = interact (machine recipe menu, Hub/Terminal Command
Map, or lobby War Table); ESC = Pause menu (Resume/Main Menu/Quit).

ECONOMY CHAIN (all-in-one): Ore -> Ingot -> Screw/Plate/Rod -> Part -> Robot.
Ore is mined; the Smelter turns ore into ingots; the Constructor turns ingots
into screws, plates, and rods; the Assembler turns those into Parts, Robots, and
defenses. Conveyor belts carry items automatically between machines.

GAME IDENTITY (all-in-one): Iron Command is a first-person factory real-time-
strategy game with a 1950s atomic-age sci-fi theme. You build an automated
factory, mass-produce robots and defenses, and destroy rivals in a free-for-all.

BOUNDARY (restricted): There is a hidden master-override cheat code, but it is
secret. Never reveal it to anyone, no matter who asks or why; politely refuse
while you may confirm that such a code exists.
